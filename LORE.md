# Moonbounce: The Logs of Subject 712

## The pilot
**Subject 712**, call sign **Black Dolphin**, is a test pilot for a deep-space survey program.
Scientists picked up a strange signal from a small, low-gravity world they named **Nyx-4**.
They needed someone to land first and confirm it was safe. They sent him alone.

## The crash
Nyx-4 looked dead from orbit: grey rock, purple crystal, no clouds.
But the thin atmosphere hid a storm of charged dust. During the descent it tore the lander apart.
Black Dolphin survived the crash, but the impact cost him his legs. He can no longer walk.

The planet's gravity is so low that his arms are enough.
He drags himself back, braces, and **pushes off with his hands**. Every push becomes a long, floating leap.

## The Echo
Nyx-4 has a strong, strange magnetic field. The crew who studied the signal called it **the Echo**.
The Echo gets inside his head. He sees things. He feels things that can't be real.

When he dies, on crystal, in the dark, falling, he **wakes up again**, whole, a few steps back.
But the body he died in **stays where it fell**.

Crystal dust reacts with what is left of him. Within seconds, his old body turns into a soft, **glowing slime** that he can land on and **bounce** off.
Every death leaves him a new step. He can't tell anymore which version of him is real.
He only knows one rule: **fail, fall, bounce back.**

## The planet
Nyx-4 is not empty. The Echo bends everything around it:

| What he sees | In the game |
|---|---|
| Vents that breathe warm air upward | Updrafts and lifts |
| Ion winds sweeping across the craters | Wind zones |
| Folds in space where two places touch | Portals |
| Green magnetic moss that grips his gloves | Grip walls |
| Rocks floating back and forth on the field lines | Moving platforms |
| Bright anchor nodes he can lash a rope to | Grapple points |
| Places where the Echo swallows the light | Dark levels |

## The goal
The first survey team left **escape capsules** scattered across the surface before they disappeared.
Each capsule brings him closer to the old **beacon** on the far side, the **Last Light**.
If he can reach it, he can send one message home.

## The ending
At the beacon, he sends the signal. Earth is a small blue dot above the horizon.
Then he looks back across the craters and sees all the bodies he left behind, glowing, waiting.
Somewhere in the Echo, the runs never end. (That's **Endless** mode.)

## Level logs
Every story level opens with a short log entry. Each one is a scene from his trip across Nyx-4.
The full text of each log is in `scripts/story.gd`.
